# Project Context

## Problem

In many rental environments, especially in emerging markets like Senegal, tenant and lease management is often handled manually or through fragmented tools (Excel, paper records, messaging apps).

This leads to:
- lack of visibility on active leases
- difficulty tracking payments and delays
- poor organization of documents
- no clear history of tenant interactions
- high dependency on individuals rather than systems

As the number of properties or tenants grows, this becomes difficult to manage and error-prone.

## Target Users

This application targets:
- small to medium property owners
- real estate managers
- individuals managing multiple rental units
- property administrators

## Proposed Solution

LocaTrack is a web application designed to centralize and structure the rental management workflow.

It provides:
- a clear structure for managing properties and tenants
- a lifecycle-based approach for lease cases (creation, active, closed)
- tracking of payments and delays
- document management per lease
- history of status changes for better traceability

## Value

The goal is to move from:
- manual and fragmented management

to:
- structured, trackable, and reliable workflows

This improves:
- operational visibility
- decision making
- data consistency
- accountability

## Scope Positioning

This project focuses on delivering a clean and modular architecture around a realistic business workflow.

It is intentionally designed as a minimal but credible system that can be extended to other domains involving case management and workflow tracking.
